// About Us

Who We Are

LabsforKidz is an educational initiative dedicated to fostering early interest in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) among young learners. We focus on delivering engaging, hands-on learning experiences that encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills in children.

Through interactive experiments, guided activities, and structured programs, LabsforKidz creates an environment where students can explore scientific concepts in a practical and accessible way. Our curriculum is designed to simplify complex ideas, making them approachable for elementary and middle school students while maintaining educational rigor.

Based in the Ellicott City area, we have already hosted our first successful community event and are actively growing. We believe every child deserves access to quality STEM education, regardless of background or resources.

Community Lab @ Miller Branch Library

Our inaugural event brought hands-on science to the Ellicott City community! We hosted a free lab experience for K–8 students at the Miller Branch Library, where kids explored science through our custom lab kits. With guided experiments and interactive learning stations, students got to dive into real STEM concepts in an engaging, pressure-free environment.
